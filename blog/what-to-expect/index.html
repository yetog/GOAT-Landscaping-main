What to Expect During a Professional Landscape Installation

If you've never hired a landscaping company before — or if you've had a bad experience in the past — knowing what the process should look like helps you make a better decision. Here's a straight walkthrough of how a professional installation runs from start to finish.

Step 1: The Free On-Site Consultation

Every project at GOAT Landscaping starts with a free consultation at your property. We walk the space with you, listen to what you want, assess what's there now, and ask questions to understand how you use the space. No pressure, no pitch — just a conversation about what's possible.

This step is non-negotiable for us. We don't quote projects we haven't seen, and we don't start work we haven't planned.

Step 2: The Written Quote

After the consultation, you receive a detailed written quote that breaks down materials, labor, and scope. You'll know exactly what you're paying for before any work is scheduled. If you have questions about the quote, we answer them — no runaround.

Step 3: Scheduling and Materials

Once you approve the quote, we get you on the schedule and source all materials. For most projects, we give you a specific start date and a realistic timeline. If anything changes (weather, material delays), we communicate proactively.

Step 4: The Installation

Our crew arrives on time, sets up properly, and works through the job systematically. For hardscape projects (pavers, patios, driveways), this typically involves base excavation, compacted gravel base, sand setting bed, and finally the paver installation and finishing. Landscape projects include grading, soil amendment, plant installation, edging, and mulching.

We don't rush. We work until each phase is done right before moving to the next one.

Step 5: Final Walkthrough and Cleanup

When installation is complete, we walk the property with you. If anything needs to be adjusted or addressed, we do it before we leave. We don't consider the job done until you're satisfied — and we clean up completely before driving away.
