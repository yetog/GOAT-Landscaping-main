About GOAT Landscaping

South Shore Long Island's custom landscaping and outdoor living company — based in Massapequa Park, NY.

Who We Are

Built to Be the Best

GOAT Landscaping is a South Shore landscaping company built around one standard: the best. Based at 5020 Sunrise Hwy in Massapequa Park, NY, we design and install custom landscapes, patios, pavers, and outdoor living spaces for homeowners who expect craftsmanship.

Not a franchise — a locally owned team. Every project gets personal attention, transparent pricing, and a crew that doesn't leave until the job is done right.

  • Fully licensed and insured in New York State
  • Locally owned — based in Massapequa Park, NY
  • 100% custom designs — no templates or shortcuts
  • Free on-site consultation before any quote
  • We don't leave until the job meets your standard
